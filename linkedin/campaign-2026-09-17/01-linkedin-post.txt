A map can show a city. Good analysis helps us ask what should happen next.

I’ve published my Cape Town Housing Opportunity Atlas: a portfolio project connecting 30 candidate precincts, 14 screening criteria and two perspectives—efficiency and spatial redress.

Explore satellite imagery, compare neighbourhoods, inspect nearby rail and facilities, and read the findings in a 30-page report with linked indexes.

The work brings together:
• Python analysis and reproducible data workflows
• GIS and interactive web mapping
• Power BI semantic modelling, star schemas and DAX
• Scenario analysis, testing and visual storytelling

It also examines rental affordability, housing delivery challenges and possible solutions. Assumptions remain visible: this is a screening study, not a development approval or a delivery forecast.
