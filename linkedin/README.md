# LinkedIn launch pack — Anthony Apollis

Open `index.html` for the poster gallery, copyable launch post, article and enquiry replies.

## Included

- 15 portrait posters, each 1080 × 1350 PNG, in `posters/`.
- `03-linkedin-carousel.pdf`: the same 15 designs as a document carousel, with selectable text.
- `01-launch-post.txt`: the primary launch post.
- `02-linkedin-article.md`: the longer portfolio case study.
- `article-cover.png`: a landscape editorial illustration for the article.
- `04-reply-templates.md`: ten reply templates and a short acknowledgement.
- `05-captions-and-alt-text.md`: matching captions and accessible descriptions for each poster.
- `editable/posters.html`: editable HTML/CSS/SVG layouts for all posters.

## Suggested use

1. Use the launch post with the PDF carousel to introduce the project and your availability.
2. Use the article with `article-cover.png` for the fuller case study.
3. Use individual PNGs for follow-up posts about a specific skill. Poster 11 focuses directly on remote availability.
4. Choose the matching caption and alt text from the supplied file.
5. Adapt an enquiry reply to the actual message before sending.
