# Replies for LinkedIn enquiries

Ready-to-use drafts. Choose the reply that fits the message and adjust any details before sending. No automatic messaging has been enabled.

## 1. General enquiry / acknowledgement

Thanks for reaching out. I’m open to remote roles and freelance or contract projects involving data analytics, GIS, Power BI modelling and visual reporting. Could you share a little about the opportunity, the main outcome you need and the expected timeline? I can then respond with the most relevant project examples.

## 2. Recruiter: remote role

Thanks for considering me. The opportunity sounds worth exploring. Could you send the role description, employment or contract arrangement, remote-location requirements, time-zone overlap and compensation range? My Cape Town Housing Opportunity Atlas demonstrates Python analysis, GIS, scenario modelling, a Power BI semantic model and illustrated reporting. I can share the project material if that would help.

## 3. Prospective client: project enquiry

Thanks — I’d be interested in learning more. What decision or deliverable do you need help with, what data is available, and what deadline and budget range are you working toward? My relevant work includes spatial analysis, reproducible Python workflows, interactive maps, BI modelling and clear reports. Once I understand the scope, we can discuss a suitable next step.

## 4. Request to see the atlas or report

Thank you for your interest. The project explores 30 Cape Town candidate precincts using 14 criteria and two ranking lenses. I can share the interactive atlas and the illustrated report. Would the technical workflow, the map experience or the report be most useful for you to review?

## 5. Positive comment on a poster

Thank you. The part I found most useful was making the reasoning visible — connecting the location, the assumptions and the comparison in one view. I’m glad the visual helped communicate it.

## 6. Question about feasibility or data quality

Good question. This is a desktop screening portfolio project. Candidate locations are points, several inputs remain estimates, and the capacity figures are modelled scenarios. Parcel boundaries, zoning, services and delivery feasibility require further verification. I’ve included those limitations and the proposed verification steps in the report.

## 7. “What are your rates?”

Thanks for asking. I’d like to understand the scope before suggesting a fee. Could you share the deliverables, data sources, deadline and expected level of collaboration? For a role or longer contract, please also share the arrangement and budget or compensation range so we can check whether there is a fit.

## 8. “When can you start?”

Thanks. Please send the proposed start date, estimated workload, time-zone overlap and the main deliverables. I’ll confirm my availability against those requirements before we agree on timing.

## 9. Relevant introduction

Thank you for thinking of me. I’m open to remote data, GIS, BI modelling and reporting work. If the person is happy to connect, please feel free to introduce us here on LinkedIn. A short note about their needs would help me share the most relevant example.

Thanks for the details. That scope goes beyond what this portfolio demonstrates, so I would want to clarify the specialist requirements before making a commitment. If there is a separate data analysis, mapping or reporting component, I’d be happy to discuss that part.
