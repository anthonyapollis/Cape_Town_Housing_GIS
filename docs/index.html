05 /Know what the evidence can support.

Ranking is a way to organise investigation. It does not establish land availability or planning rights.

How the rankings work

The existing model normalises fourteen criteria across these 30 precincts and combines them using two sets of weights. Efficiency prioritises job accessibility and transit. The redress lens adds spatial-redress and displacement assumptions.

Scores are relative to this candidate set. They are not feasibility percentages. Job accessibility is a gravity model using eight employment nodes and assumed job weights, not measured journey times.

What the new geography adds

Nearest mapped station, raw school and health-feature counts within 1 km and 2 km, and nearest-facility locations are calculated from the saved OSM snapshot. These counts differ from the original weighted social-facility index. Some OSM features can refer to the same campus.

What still needs verification

  • Parcel extent, ownership, tenure, availability and current zoning.
  • Floodlines, environmental constraints, contamination and infrastructure capacity.
  • Actual walking routes, public-transport operations and facility capacity.
  • Occupancy, displacement risk, restitution status and community priorities.

Area, density, land value and job weights are inputs rather than independently verified measurements. Eight qualitative criteria are explicitly desktop estimates. The heat and vegetation indicators are estimates too.

The legacy 3D view and tranche dates are conceptual. They do not represent surveyed buildings, confirmed rights or a funded delivery programme.
