Evidence for investigation.

This is a desktop screening of a supplied candidate set. Precinct points are not parcel boundaries; assumed zoning, area and capacity require verification. The report distinguishes mapped measurements, model derivations and analyst estimates, with a practical programme for closing the gaps.
